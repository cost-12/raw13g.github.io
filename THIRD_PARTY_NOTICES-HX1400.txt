ps4-fan-threshold (includes/payloads/Tools/fan-thresholds/)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Payloads for Syscon thermal threshold management (60 °C, 70 °C, 80 °C, 85 °C).
Derived from open-source PS4 Fan Control utilities.
Binaries differ exclusively at offset 0x2318 (target temperature byte).
